Know before you pay

Plain answers to the questions everyone asks.

How to buy property in Zimbabwe from abroad

Fix your budget and what you want, shortlist from our live market, commission a verification report on the property you like, then move the money through proper channels (banks and registered transfer methods — never cash to individuals). We walk you to the Deeds Office transfer and hand over the keys. Most transactions run 4–8 weeks once the report clears.

What "checked on the ground" actually means

A person we're accountable to stands on the property: physical state, water and power, access and boundaries, neighbours, and the paperwork — title deeds, rates and taxes, permits, and any encumbrances on the title. You get photos, video and the documents, plus a written report stating exactly what we confirmed and what we couldn't.

Building in Zimbabwe without being there

Remote builds fail when nobody on site answers to the owner. Our build management takes that job: vetted builders, materials bought and checked, payments released stage by stage, and photo reports after every stage — for 10% of the build cost. You approve each stage before the next one is paid.
