PROPERTY GUIDES & UPDATES

Understand Zimbabwe property before you commit

Straightforward information about buying from abroad, checking documents and understanding property risks in Zimbabwe.

Buying property in Zimbabwe from abroad

Start with your budget, preferred location and the type of property you need. Ask for the property's ownership documents and arrange independent checks before paying a deposit. Confirm the transaction process with a qualified Zimbabwean legal practitioner and use properly documented payment and transfer arrangements. Do not assume a listing or document has been verified unless the checks and findings are clearly explained to you.

What to check before buying a stand

Do not judge a stand by its price or pegs alone. Ask what type of land it is, who is authorised to sell it, what ownership or allocation documents exist, whether subdivision and development approvals apply, and whether the stand has access to roads and services. Rural, peri-urban and urban land can have different legal and planning requirements. Get the relevant documents checked by qualified professionals before you pay.

Considering a mine or mining claim?

A mine is a business as well as a site. Before investing, ask for the relevant mining rights and permits, ownership and transfer records, production history, operating costs, equipment and liabilities, safety position, and available financial records. Site visits and independent technical, legal and financial reviews can help you understand what is being offered. Past output or profit claims should be supported by evidence and do not guarantee future returns.
