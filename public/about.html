About Homeland Property Group

ABOUT HOMELAND PROPERTY GROUP

Your property partner in Zimbabwe.

Homeland Property Group helps people discover, buy, sell and rent property across Zimbabwe. We bring property opportunities and practical support together in one place.

We work across residential, commercial, land and selected specialist property categories. Whether you are searching for a family home, a stand, a rental property, a farm or an investment opportunity, our team can help you explore listings and understand the process. We also work with property owners, developers and industry professionals to bring suitable properties to market.

Our approach is built on clear information, responsive service and professional conduct. We aim to make property enquiries and transactions easier to understand, while encouraging clients to complete the appropriate legal and ownership checks before making commitments. That single fact shapes every recommendation we make, including the times we tell you not to buy.

Our focus

Connecting clients with property opportunities across Zimbabwe, from residential homes and stands to commercial and specialist properties.

Our approach

We value accurate property information, transparent communication and clear agreement on the services and terms involved.

Professional support

We encourage clients to use appropriately qualified estate agents, legal practitioners, valuers and conveyancers where required.

Property transactions should be completed in accordance with applicable Zimbabwean laws and regulations. Clients should obtain independent legal and professional advice where appropriate.
