About Homeland Property Group

ABOUT HOMELAND PROPERTY GROUP

Your people on the ground.

Homeland Property Group exists for one person: the Zimbabwean abroad who wants to own something real back home — and wants to sleep at night while it happens.

We are a one-stop property partner. We sell our own verified listings. We source properties we don't have. We verify anything before you pay for it. And we manage your purchase or your build while you watch from abroad — with weekly reports, staged payments and receipts for everything.

Portals serve agents. Agents serve sellers. We serve you — the buyer — and we're the only party in the chain paid by you. That single fact shapes every recommendation we make, including the times we tell you not to buy.

Why we started

Because too many Zimbabweans abroad have sent money home for a stand, a house or a claim — and received a story instead of a title deed. We built the checks we wished our own families had.

How we are paid

By you, the buyer, and by nobody else. No hidden seller kickbacks on your purchase. If a property does not stand up to our checks, we say so and you keep your money.

How we work

Every purchase closes through registered estate agents and conveyancers under Zimbabwean law, with funds held in a conveyancer's trust account — never a personal one, not even ours.

All property transactions close through registered estate agents and conveyancers in line with Zimbabwean law. Homeland Property Group acts as your consultant and owner's representative throughout.
